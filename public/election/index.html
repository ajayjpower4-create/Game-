Draft anyone. Run the campaign. Call the race.

Any two people. One election.

Pick your candidates — famous or invented — schedule three months of rallies, decide what they shout from the podium, then either rig the map yourself or let the AI call all 51 contests.

A fictional game. Every result here is invented for play — it is not a poll, a forecast, or a claim about any real person or election.

Step 1 · Draft your candidates

Pick a famous name and the game already knows who they are. Invent someone and you'll need to write them into existence.

Step 2 · Three months of rallies

Book each candidate into states across the campaign window. Where they go moves those states — and late rallies hit hardest.

Step 3 · What do they say up there?

The stump speech every rally is built around. Pick the themes they hammer and write the line the crowd chants back.

Step 4 · Election day

The polls are closed. How does this end?

Step 5 · Call the states

Click a state to hand it to your first candidate, click again for the second, once more to clear it.
