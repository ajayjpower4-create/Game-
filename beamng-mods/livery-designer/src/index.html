Any vehicle works: a modded vehicle from your mods folder, or one of the game's own from BeamNG.drive\content\vehicles.

The app finds the vehicle's template, paint and Paint Design slot by itself. When you're done, it makes the mod file for you to drop into your mods folder.

Template (UV map)

Left & right sides

Only tick this if mirrored text shows up backwards in the game.

Sheets

Most vehicles need one sheet. Trucks and some mods use separate textures for other parts (cab, box, trailer). Add a sheet for each one.
